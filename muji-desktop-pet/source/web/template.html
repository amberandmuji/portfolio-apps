Muji · Desktop Pom

A cosy pixel-art Pomeranian who lives along the bottom of your Mac screen. This page is a browser preview. He's down there now.

Muji is saying hi
●●●●○ energy

What he does

  • Wanders, sniffs, sits, looks at you, yawns, stretches, and does the odd zoomies.
  • Gets tired and naps, curled up or belly-up. Sleepier in the evening, asleep 10:30pm–7am.
  • Step away for 5 minutes and he curls up. Come back and he stretches and says hi.
then move the mouse to come back

Get him on your Mac

macOS 13+. Needs Xcode or the Command Line Tools (xcode-select --install).

cd source
bash build.sh
open build/PomPet.app

The overlay is click-through and shows on every Space. Paw icon in the menu bar: status, size, hide, walk on the Dock, open at login.
